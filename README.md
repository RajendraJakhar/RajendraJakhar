## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏫 1. RNT Borwa — Complete School ERP System

A full-stack School ERP system developed for RNT School Borwa, combining a public-facing school website with a comprehensive administrative management platform.

The system brings multiple school management operations together through a centralized admin panel.

#### Key Features

**Student & Staff Management**
- Student management
- Staff and administration management
- Student history and academic records

**Academic Management**
- Homework management
- Attendance management
- Results and marksheet management
- Student promotions

**Administrative Management**
- Admissions management
- Fee management
- Master data management
- ID card management
- Notifications

**School Information**
- Alumni and toppers management
- Public school website
- School information, gallery, and notices

**Technologies:** Next.js, React, Supabase, Firebase, Tailwind CSS

🔗 **[Visit RNT Borwa](https://rntborwa.in/)**

### 🌐 2. Vaatira — Digital Growth Platform

A modern website developed for Vaatira, a digital growth agency focused on matchmaking and relationship coaching businesses.

The project combines a professional public-facing website with administrative functionality.

#### Key Features

- Modern and responsive website
- Professional digital presence
- Admin panel for platform management
- Full-stack web development
- Database integration

**Technologies:** Next.js, React, Supabase, Firebase, Tailwind CSS
