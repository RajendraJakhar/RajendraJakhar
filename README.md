## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏫 RNT Borwa — Complete School ERP System

A full-stack School ERP system developed for RNT School Borwa, combining a public-facing school website with a comprehensive administrative management platform.

The system brings multiple school operations together through a centralized admin panel.

**Key Features**

- Student and staff management
- Admissions and attendance management
- Homework and academic history
- Results and marksheet management
- Fee management and student promotions
- Master data and ID card management
- Alumni and toppers management
- Notifications and administrative access management

**Technologies:** Next.js, React, Supabase, Firebase, Tailwind CSS
